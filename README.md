## What I work on

- **Anuvadini AI Translation & OCR System**: Python and FastAPI backend services that use Tesseract OCR and PyTorch to extract text from scanned PDFs and translate it in real time. The translation tool is live on the official Anuvadini website, and the OCR solution was delivered to the Parliament of India for legacy document digitization.
- **GIS Water Bodies Analysis Tool**: a monitoring platform for the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs that analyzes 10+ years of Sentinel satellite data to track water bodies and land cover, with 10,000+ automated reports delivered.
- **Eco-Club Data Platform**: a blockchain-based verification system that government schools use to log environmental activities across 7 sustainability themes.
- **AICTE National Internship Portal** and **India Maritime Week 2027 Portal**: React and TypeScript frontend work with Node.js and MySQL backend updates on live national portals.

> Most of this work lives in private organization repositories.

## Featured project

**[Dental Equipment Detection](https://github.com/Nitsh07/dental_equipments_anuvadini)**: a YOLOv8 model fine-tuned on 14 dental instrument classes, with CLAHE low-light enhancement, temporal voting and IoU tracking to cut false positives, and automated PDF inspection reports.
